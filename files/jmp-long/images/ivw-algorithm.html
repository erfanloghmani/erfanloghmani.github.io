Algorithm Bias-corrected inverse-variance combination (IVW)
Input: observational data $\mathcal{D}_O$; experimental pairs $\mathcal{D}_E$; evaluation pairs $\mathcal{P}$
Output: combined win-probability $\hat\theta_{\mathrm{new}}(a,b)$ for each pair in $\mathcal{P}$
  1. Fit CTR-prediction model $\hat m_O$ on $\mathcal{D}_O$ ▹ biased, low-variance branch
  2. Fit logistic model $\hat g_E$ on $\mathcal{D}_E$ ▹ unbiased, high-variance branch
  4. ▹ Estimate the bias of the observational branch by cross-fitting
  5. Partition $\mathcal{D}_E$ into halves $\mathcal{D}_E^{1}$, $\mathcal{D}_E^{2}$
  6. for $k \in \{1,2\}$ do
  7. Train logistic $\hat g_{-k}$ on the other half $\mathcal{D}_E^{\,3-k}$
  8. For each pair in $\mathcal{D}_E^{k}$, form $p^O$ (from $\hat m_O$) and $p^E$ (from $\hat g_{-k}$)
  9. $\widehat{b}_k \gets \mathrm{mean}\big(p^O - p^E\big)$ over those pairs
  10. $\widehat{b} \gets \tfrac{1}{2}\big(\widehat{b}_1 + \widehat{b}_2\big)$ ▹ estimated bias of $\hat\theta_B$
  12. for each pair $(a,b)\in\mathcal{P}$ do
  13. $(\hat\theta_U, \widehat{\mathrm{Var}}_U) \gets$ win-probability and variance from $\hat g_E$
  14. $(\hat\theta_B, \widehat{\mathrm{Var}}_B) \gets$ Monte-Carlo win-probability and variance from $\hat m_O$ ▹ CTR $\sim$ Normal
  15. $w^\star \gets \widehat{\mathrm{Var}}_U \big/ \big(\widehat{b}^{\,2} + \widehat{\mathrm{Var}}_B + \widehat{\mathrm{Var}}_U\big)$
  16. $\hat\theta_{\mathrm{new}}(a,b) \gets w^\star\,\hat\theta_B + (1-w^\star)\,\hat\theta_U$
  17. return $\{\hat\theta_{\mathrm{new}}(a,b) : (a,b)\in\mathcal{P}\}$
