IV estimation
Instrumental variables
  • Consistent under endogeneity
  • Typically higher variance
vs
OLS
Ordinary least squares
  • Lower variance (more efficient)
  • Can be biased if confounders remain
Hausman test
If IV and OLS differ
→ bias is large → use IV
If IV and OLS agree
→ little bias → use OLS (less variance)
Limitation

This chooses which estimator to use — a discrete selection. Our question: can we combine the two instead of picking one?
